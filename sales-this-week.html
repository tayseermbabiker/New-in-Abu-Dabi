Frequently Asked Questions

How often is this page updated?

This page is checked and updated weekly. Each deal shows the date it was last verified and the date it expires, so you can always see how current the information is.

Where does this sales information come from?

Every deal is manually checked against the retailer's own website, app, or official social media before it's listed here. We link to the original source for each deal so you can verify it yourself.

Why isn't a deal I saw in-store listed here?

Many store promotions in Abu Dhabi are only announced via SMS or WhatsApp broadcasts to existing customers and aren't posted publicly. We list what we can verify from official retailer sources; if you know of a current sale we've missed, let us know via the contact page.

Do expired deals stay on this page?

No. Deals are automatically removed from this page once their listed end date passes, so you only see offers that are still valid.
